# Opportunity Card — emitted verbatim, one per ranked opportunity

> Emitted by `expansion-finder` for every candidate ranked in the top 10 or above $25k
> Opportunity ARR. **Internal document.** It carries propensity, ranked value, health band and
> CSM hours, none of which may appear in anything the customer reads — the customer's version
> is `expansion-one-pager.md`.
>
> Every slot is filled from data with a provenance tag, or printed as
> `UNKNOWN — requires <source>`. Nothing is left as an unfilled placeholder and nothing is
> guessed; a default that was used instead of a value goes in the Assumption Register of the
> parent pipeline artifact.
