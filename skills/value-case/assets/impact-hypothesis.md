## The six tests before this is agreed

- [ ] **Named team**, not "the business" — you cannot baseline an organisation
- [ ] **A system**, not a survey — name the object and the field, and store the query
- [ ] **A customer verifier** who will re-run it at the measurement date without us
- [ ] **Unit economics** attached, or this can never become money
- [ ] **One to three** hypotheses total; past three, none gets instrumented properly
- [ ] **A measurement date 90 days before the opt-out deadline** (`R1`), not before the renewal date
