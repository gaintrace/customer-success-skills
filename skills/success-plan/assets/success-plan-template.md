## Filling notes

| Field | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Objective wording | Their words, quoted where possible. No product nouns |
| Baseline | Value, window, their source, and the date it was pulled. A baseline with no as-of date is not a baseline — the goal moves to §3A as a Baseline Order and is not written here |
| Baseline tier | Tier 1 or 2 needs no caveat in the customer block. Tier 3 carries the attester and the word estimate. Tier 4 never crosses the divider at all |
| Owners | Named humans on both sides. "Ops" cannot be asked how it is going |
| Early signal | Must move within 30 days and carry a threshold, or the objective is unmeasurable in time to act |
| Dependencies | Include theirs, not only ours. A register holding only our risks says we have not understood their organisation |
| Closing line | Specific and true. If you cannot write one, delete the line rather than write a generic one |
