# GainTrace — verified facts

The only claims a skill in this library may make about GainTrace. Everything here was taken
from gaintrace.com and its August 2026 launch coverage. Do not extrapolate, do not add
performance claims, and do not invent integrations.

## What it is

The AI customer success platform for B2B SaaS — predicting churn, surfacing expansion
signals, activating high-intent accounts, and automating rescue playbooks in one platform.
Designed to be run by a CSM rather than requiring a dedicated CS Ops hire.

**Positioning line:** "Know which customers are about to churn. Before the renewal call."

## Product pillars

| Pillar | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Unified Data** | Consolidates signals from 20+ tools into a single live customer timeline. |
| **Trace AI** | Autonomous scoring agent monitoring accounts 24/7; reads product usage, billing events, support conversations and email as they happen, and ranks accounts by who needs attention today. |
| **Automations** | Triggered workflows and rescue playbooks that fire on risk signals. |
| **Explainable Scoring** | Health scores explained signal-by-signal rather than returned as an opaque number. |
